STORY 2: The Girl Who Married A Ghost Because Of Jollof Rice

My name is Lovie. And I am not a normal girl.

Every night by 12am, I used to sneak to the kitchen to eat leftover jollof rice. Not small jollof o — the party jollof wey my mama cook for Sunday, the one wey get smoky smell and chicken wey dey call your name.

One night, I tiptoe go kitchen. I open pot. The jollof don finish! I shout, "WHO CHOP MY JOLLOF?!"

Then a voice from behind the pot say, "Na me chop am. Sorry."

I turn. I see a FINE BOY sitting on top of the fridge! Fine pass anime boy! White hair, glowing eyes, wearing white agbada like Oba. But his leg... e no touch ground. E dey float!

I scream, "GHOST!!!" He say, "Shhh! If you scream again, your mama go wake up and beat both of us."

He say, "My name is Kaito. I be ghost prince. I die 200 years ago because I chop jollof wey too sweet and I laugh die. Since then, my spirit dey find the perfect jollof to free me. And your mama jollof... na the one."

For 7 days, every night, me and Kaito dey cook together. He go dey float, dey cut onion with magic, onion no go enter my eye again. He go dey sing anime song while I dey stir pot.

Day 1, we burn rice. Day 2, we put salt instead of sugar. Day 3, we cook rice wey turn to tuwo. Day 4, my mama catch us! She see pot dey float for air. She shout, "Lovie! You don join witchcraft?!" Mama carry broom pursue Kaito round house!

Day 6, I cook the best jollof of my life. I put love inside. I write "Kaito + Lovie" with carrot on top of the rice.

Day 7, he supposed to go. Heaven don open, angels dey call am. But he look me, he look jollof, he look me again... He say, "Heaven get jollof?" Angel say, "No." He say, "Then I no dey go. I go stay here with my wife."

I shout, "WIFE?! WHO BE YOUR WIFE?!" He say, "You. You cook for me 7 days. For my village, that na marriage."

Na so I marry ghost because of jollof rice. Now every night, he dey help me cook, he dey help me wash plate, and when NEPA take light, he go glow make I see road.

And my mama? She don accept am. She say, "At least ghost husband no dey cheat, no dey smoke, and e no dey chop my meat — e only dey chop jollof."

THE END
