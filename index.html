STORY 2: The Girl Who Married A Ghost Because Of Jollof Rice

My name is Lovie. And I am not a normal girl.

Every night by 12am, I used to sneak to the kitchen to eat leftover jollof rice. Not small jollof o — the party jollof wey my mama cook for Sunday.

One night, I tiptoe go kitchen. I open pot. The jollof don finish! I shout, "WHO CHOP MY JOLLOF?!"

Then a voice from behind the pot say, "Na me chop am. Sorry."

I turn. I see a FINE BOY sitting on top of the fridge! Fine pass anime boy! White hair, glowing eyes, wearing white agbada like Oba. But his leg... e no touch ground. E dey float!

I scream, "GHOST!!!" He say, "Shhh! If you scream again, your mama go wake up and beat both of us."

He say, "My name is Kaito. I be ghost prince. I die 200 years ago because I chop jollof wey too sweet and I laugh die."

For 7 days, every night, me and Kaito dey cook together. He go dey float, dey cut onion with magic, onion no go enter my eye again.

Day 1, we burn rice. Day 2, we put salt instead of sugar. Day 3, we cook rice wey turn to tuwo. Day 4, my mama catch us! She see pot dey float for air. She shout, "Lovie! You don join witchcraft?!" Mama carry broom pursue Kaito round house!

Day 6, I cook the best jollof of my life. I put love inside. I write "Kaito + Lovie" with carrot on top of the rice.

Day 7, he supposed to go. Heaven don open, angels dey call am. But he look me, he look jollof, he look me again... He say, "Heaven get jollof?" Angel say, "No." He say, "Then I no dey go. I go stay here with my wife."

I shout, "WIFE?! WHO BE YOUR WIFE?!" He say, "You. You cook for me 7 days. For my village, that na marriage."

Na so I marry ghost because of jollof rice. Now every night, he dey help me cook, he dey help me wash plate, and when NEPA take light, he go glow make I see road.

And my mama? She don accept am. She say, "At least ghost husband no dey cheat, no dey smoke, and e no dey chop my meat — e only dey chop jollof."

THE END

My Crush Thought I Was A Witch Because I Sneezed My name is Amara. I am 17 years old. I am in SS2. And I have a big problem. My problem name is Chidi. Chidi is the finest boy in our school. Tall, dark, quiet, always reading manga for corner. When he walks, all the girls go, "Hmmmm!" When he talks, even teacher go forget wetin she dey teach. And me? I am the girl wey dey always fall inside gutter in front of him. Every time! One day, I decide, "Today, I go talk to Chidi. Today, I go be brave girl like anime main character!" I wear my best uniform. I iron am well well. I put small lip gloss — the one wey my mama say make I no put. I carry my lunch — jollof rice with two meat, because I wan share with am. I walk to his class. My heart dey beat like drum — gbim gbim gbim gbim! I reach him. I open mouth to say, "Hi Chidi, I..." Then... Achooo!!! My Queen, I sneeze with full power! The sneeze carry my wig — sorry, I mean my hair — my hair fly comot! My lip gloss fly enter his book! My jollof rice fly go up, come down for his head! Everybody for class shout, "AHHHH!!!" Chidi look me. He look jollof for him head. He look my hair for ground. He say with serious face: "...Are you a witch?" I say, "Wetin?!" He say, "Only witch fit sneeze and cause earthquake. My grandmother tell me." I begin cry. I run comot for class. I go hide for toilet, dey cry, dey eat the remaining jollof wey fall for ground. Yes, I eat am. Waste no food! After school, I dey trek go house, vexing. Then I hear, "Amara! Wait!" Na Chidi! He dey run after me with my hair for hand, and my lunch box. He say, "Sorry. I no mean to call you witch. I was just... shy. I no sabi how to talk to fine girl." I say, "Fine girl? Me? The girl wey sneeze jollof for your head?" He laugh. His laugh sweet pass malt. He say, "Yes. You. Since JSS1, I dey watch you. You be the only girl wey dey laugh loud for class, wey dey fight for last meat for dining hall, wey dey fall but you go stand up dey laugh again. I like you." My leg begin shake. My heart begin do break dance. He say, "Can I... Can I walk you home? And tomorrow, can I buy you another jollof? Because I chop the one wey fall for my head... E sweet." I say, "You chop jollof wey fall for your head?!" He say, "Na the sweetest jollof I don ever chop." From that day, Chidi dey walk me home every day. He dey carry my bag. He dey buy me Fan Ice every Friday. He dey help me do assignment, but he no go let me copy — he go say, "Amara, try yourself, you sabi am." One day, rain fall. Heavy rain! We run enter one small shop to hide. No umbrella. Everywhere cold. Chidi remove his sweater, give me. The sweater smell like him — like rain and old manga book. He look me, he say, "Amara... if I sneeze and my shoe fly comot, you go still like me?" I say, "Chidi, if you sneeze and your leg fly comot, I go still like you." He smile. He hold my hand small small. My hand dey sweat, his hand dey sweat, our sweat mix together — sweet sweat! Then NEPA take light. Everywhere dark. He whisper, "Amara, I like you. Like... like more than manga. More than jollof. More than..." I whisper, "More than what?" He say, "More than my PS5." My Queen, THAT ONE NA REAL LOVE! Boy wey love you pass PS5, marry am! Now we be best couple for school. People dey call us "Sneeze Couple." When I sneeze, whole school go shout, "AMARA DON SNEEZE AGAIN!" And Chidi go come, carry my hair, carry my bag, and say, "My witch, let's go home." And me? I no dey shame again. Because the sneeze wey I think go disgrace me, na that sneeze bring my sweet love.
